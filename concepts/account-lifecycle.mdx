Every account you add to Warmr moves through a predictable lifecycle, from its first login to a steady posting rotation. Warmr manages each transition for you: a new account can be held back from posting for a ramp period you configure, then joins the rotation, and keeps running light activity between posts rather than sitting dormant. You can shorten, lengthen, or skip the ramp per account.

## The five lifecycle stages

<Steps>
  <Step title="Fresh">
    A fresh account is a brand-new login that you've just added to Warmr. At this stage, the account has no posting history and no established trust signals with the platform. Warmr deliberately blocks posting for fresh accounts. Your content queue will build up, but nothing goes out until the account matures. This is not a bug; it's the single most important guardrail Warmr provides.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ramped in">
    Once Warmr begins its daily ramp sessions, the account enters the ramping-up stage. Warmr drives your real iPhone to scroll feeds, like content, follow accounts, and run searches, every day, on a realistic cadence. These actions accumulate trust signals that tell the platform this is an active, genuine user. See [Ramp](/concepts/account-ramp) for a full breakdown of each action.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Matured">
    After sufficient ramp activity, Warmr determines the account has passed the platform's trust thresholds and marks it as matured. A matured account joins your active posting rotation. The exact time this takes varies by platform and account history. Warmr monitors the signals automatically and advances the account when it's ready.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Kept active">
    Maturity isn't a destination. It's a state you maintain. Accounts that aren't scheduled to post can keep running a light daily activity session, so they are not sitting completely idle between posting periods. This is configurable per account and can be turned off.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Posting">
    When an account enters a posting slot, Warmr doesn't just upload your content and move on. Every post cycle is wrapped in ramp activity: the account engages naturally before the post goes out, and again afterward. This makes each posting event look like a natural moment in a busy user's day, not a robotic upload.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Never force a fresh account into posting before it matures. Warmr blocks this automatically, but bypassing the ramp phase dramatically increases ban risk.
</Warning>

<Note>
  The time it takes an account to move from fresh to matured varies by platform and the age of the account. Newer accounts on stricter platforms like TikTok typically require longer ramp windows than older accounts on more lenient platforms. Warmr monitors trust signals continuously and advances each account when, and only when, it's ready. You don't need to track this manually.
</Note>

For a detailed look at what happens during the ramp phase and why each action matters, see [Account Ramp](/concepts/account-ramp).
