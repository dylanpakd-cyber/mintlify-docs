Warmr supports X (formerly Twitter) and LinkedIn for account ramp, keeping accounts active, healthy, and free from dormancy flags. If you manage these accounts alongside your TikTok and Instagram racks, Warmr runs ramp activity on all platforms in parallel so nothing goes cold.

## What Warmr does on X

Warmr drives the X iOS app with the same real-tap approach it uses on every other platform. There is no unofficial API and no browser automation involved.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Timeline Activity" icon="scroll">
    Warmr scrolls your timeline, likes posts, and follows accounts on a daily schedule to keep the account signaling as an active user.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Search & Discovery" icon="search">
    Warmr uses X's search and discovery features to diversify activity patterns and reduce repetition flags.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## What Warmr does on LinkedIn

LinkedIn ramp follows the same pattern: real taps, real app, real activity signals.

## Device setup

Up to **8 accounts per platform**. X and LinkedIn are **activity-only** today (no auto-posting), so they run alongside your TikTok and Instagram accounts on the same devices.

## Account ramp schedule

X and LinkedIn accounts move through the same ramp schedule as TikTok and Instagram. Warmr brings each account from fresh through its ramp, then keeps it active alongside whatever you post manually.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Fresh">
    The account is newly added to Warmr. Daily ramp activity begins immediately.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ramped in">
    Warmr has established a regular activity history: scrolling, liking, following.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Matured">
    The account has passed the maturity threshold and is in a healthy, active state.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Kept active">
    Warmr continues daily activity indefinitely, so the account never goes dormant between your manual posts.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  X and LinkedIn ramp works the same way as TikTok and Instagram ramp: real iPhone taps, no unofficial APIs. The difference is that Warmr runs activity on these platforms but does not publish to them; you post to X and LinkedIn yourself.
</Note>

## Capacity

Each iPhone can run ramp for up to **8 accounts per platform**. You can run X and LinkedIn ramp on the same devices as your TikTok and Instagram accounts. Warmr interleaves activity across platforms automatically.

## Layout updates

When X or LinkedIn ships an app update that moves a button or changes a screen layout, Warmr's tap-based approach detects the change immediately. A patch ships within **24–48 hours** and your Mac app pulls it automatically on its next check-in.

<Tip>
  Use Warmr to keep your X and LinkedIn accounts active while you post manually or through a dedicated scheduling tool. A consistently active account performs better even when you post by hand. Algorithms on both platforms deprioritize dormant accounts regardless of post quality.
</Tip>
