FAQ
Before
you start.
What people ask before they subscribe. Have another? Join the Discord .
I just set up a new account. Can I post from it right away? + You can, but Warmr defaults to easing in instead. A new account starts on a light activity schedule you configure and builds to full posting volume over the number of days you choose, then joins your posting rotation. You set the ramp length and the daily volume. Works on TikTok and Instagram.
How much of this can Warmr actually control? + No tool can promise outcomes, and anyone who does is lying. What Warmr controls is execution: it drives iPhones you own, performs the upload steps reliably, paces activity to the schedule you set, and logs every action. What you control is your content, your posting cadence, and your network setup.
What happens when an app changes its layout? + Warmr reads each platform from real screenshots, and we ship a patch within 24 to 48 hours when a layout shifts. Your app pulls it on the next check-in. You rebuild nothing.
What if a device hangs mid-upload? + Warmr catches the stall, restarts the iPhone-side process, and resumes from the same account. Nothing double-posts, because state is checkpointed to disk. Even if your Mac reboots, the run picks up from the last checkpoint.
Do I need proxies? + Most operators give each account its own mobile or residential IP, a 4G proxy, or its own SIM, rather than running everything through one home connection. Warmr paces the on-device activity; the network setup is yours to choose. We walk through it on the call.
Where does my data live? + On the Mac you own, on your own network: accounts, devices, and the activity log all stay local. Cloud Drop just queues your uploads; once your Mac pulls a clip, your credentials and source files never leave home.
Who is this for? + Solo founders, indie operators, and one-person growth teams publishing to TikTok and Instagram at volume, with X and LinkedIn kept active between manual posts. The builder who'd rather plug in three iPhones than hire three VAs. Start with one, add more when you're ready.
What does Warmr not do? + It doesn't buy or sell accounts, scrape inboxes, touch unofficial APIs, or break platform rules. It paces, runs, and logs every action on real devices you own.
How do I get access? + Start free for 7 days: sign in with Google, get your license key in seconds, no card. Install the Mac app, paste the key, and your first scheduled run starts in under 10 minutes.
